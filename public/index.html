    02 · About

    From engineering to experience

    UI/UX Designer | Product Designer | Spatial & XR Interaction

    I didn't start in design. I started in engineering. Then infrastructure. Then XR. Somewhere along the way, I realized the thread connecting all of it was the same thing: making complex systems feel simple to the person using them.

    Now I do that deliberately, with hands-on experience building both mainstream digital interfaces and spatial XR frameworks. Designing at the intersection of psychology, art, and engineering logic, I specialize in data-dense, intuitive interfaces: the kind that reward attention to detail and hold up under real use.

    My process lives between structured Figma systems (Auto Layout, components, variables) and real-time engines like Unity, which means I think about design both as craft and as code. Backed by a Google UX Certification, I architect user-centered layouts, interactive prototypes, and immersive XR experiences that are as functionally rigorous as they are visually sharp.

    My engineering roots mean I think about constraints, not just aesthetics.

    My design instincts mean I never lose sight of the person on the other side of the screen.

    The journey

    1. EngineeringElectronics & Communication Engineering (ECE). Learned to think in systems and constraints.
    2. Enterprise ITStorage and backup administration. Lived inside heavy, data-dense enterprise tools every day.
    3. XR DevelopmentBuilt spatial interfaces in Unity with C# and the XR Interaction Toolkit.
    4. UI/UX & ProductGoogle UX Certified. Now designing interfaces that keep complex systems clear and fast.
